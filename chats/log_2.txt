  We Radio (`we_radio`) is a cross-platform Flutter internet radio streaming application backed by a Python Flask API. It lets users browse, search, filter, favorite, and stream radio stations from a MariaDB catalog while persisting personal favorites and user-added stations locally in SQLite.

Operative Nature:
